ABOUT

About Cashel Fitzgerald

Cashel Fitzgerald is the co-founder and CTO of Zomma, where he builds computer-use agents for financial operations. Based in San Francisco, he works on machine learning systems that connect models with real software and workflows.

What I work on

At Zomma, I build computer-use agents for the financial back office. These agents interact with the screens and tools people already use. My work brings together machine learning and the engineering needed to make models useful in real workflows.

My independent projects explore reinforcement learning, visual speech recognition, and language models. This site brings those projects together with their source material and an interactive look inside a running diffusion language model.

Background

My background includes computer science at Cornell, electrical and computer engineering at UT Austin, and previous work at Modern Intelligence. I am based in San Francisco, California.

Selected work

Marvin makes token states and measured model activations visible during generation. My speaker-adaptation research with Jihyun Kim investigates how a lip-reading model can adapt to a video without test-time gradient updates. Minecraft RL explores agent training in a custom combat environment.
